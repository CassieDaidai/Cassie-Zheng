## Experience

### 4A / Communication Groups

I started my career in advertising agencies and communication groups including BlueFocus, McCann, and Social Touch.

My work covered integrated marketing proposals, campaign planning, brand strategy, creative direction, and project execution for consumer brands across multiple industries.

This stage trained my core strategy muscles: reading briefs, diagnosing brand problems, finding consumer insight, and turning strategy into executable communication plans.

### Platform

I later joined Xiaohongshu as a commercial marketing expert, focusing on beauty and personal care brands in South China.

I worked with and supported projects for P&G brands including Olay, Safeguard, Pantene, and SK-II, with a focus on platform content strategy, IP resources, creator collaboration, and social growth paths.

This stage helped me understand how content platforms really work: why users search, how content gets discovered, and how brands move from exposure to trust and conversion.

### Brand Side

Most recently, I worked as Head of New Media for 8+Minutes, a fast-growing Chinese haircare brand.

I built Xiaohongshu content systems from 0 to 1, including creator collaboration, content templates, seeding mechanisms, sentiment monitoring, and data review workflows.

This stage brought me closer to real brand operations: content is not only expression, but also part of product rhythm, user trust, and business growth.

## Current Focus

The question I am exploring now:

How can AI enter the full marketing chain?

```text
Research → Insight → Strategy → Content → Distribution → Measurement
```

To me, AI is not only a tool for writing faster. It can change how marketers organize information, find insight, generate strategic options, build content systems, and improve through feedback.

I am also interested in a larger question: when a marketer's experience becomes structured, searchable, and reusable, can it become a new kind of personal asset?
